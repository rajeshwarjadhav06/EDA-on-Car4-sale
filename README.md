# 🚗 Used Car Market Analysis (EDA Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the **used car market in India** using data collected from Carz 4 Sale. The goal is to uncover **pricing trends, customer behavior, and market patterns** using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
The used car market in India is highly fragmented. Buyers and sellers often struggle to determine the **right price** due to multiple influencing factors such as:
- Car brand  
- Manufacturing year  
- Fuel type  
- Ownership history  
- Location  

This leads to **pricing inefficiencies and confusion in decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze factors affecting used car prices  
- Compare car prices across cities and states  
- Identify popular car brands and models  
- Understand impact of fuel type and ownership  
- Discover demand trends in the market

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

### 🔹 Univariate Analysis
- Most cars fall in the lower price range  
- Few luxury cars increase the average price  
- Highest number of cars manufactured around 2023  
- Majority cars are:
  - Single Owner (72%)  
  - Second Owner (28%)  

---

### 🔹 Categorical Insights
- Top states with highest listings:
  - Karnataka  
  - Maharashtra  
  - Uttar Pradesh  

- Top cities:
  - Bangalore  
  - Chennai  
  - Mumbai  

- Most popular brands:
  - Maruti Suzuki  
  - Hyundai  
  - Tata  

---

### 🔹 Bivariate Analysis
- Car prices increase with newer manufacturing years  
- Ownership impact:
  - Single-owner cars → Higher price  
  - Second-owner cars → Lower price  

- Price variation by city:
  - High price cities → Bangalore, Hyderabad  
  - Low price cities → Balotra, Vadodara  

---

### 🔹 Key Findings
- 🚘 Newer cars have higher resale value  
- 👤 First-owner cars are more expensive and preferred  
- 🌆 Metro cities have higher car prices  
- 🏷️ Mid-range cars dominate the market (₹45K – ₹20L)  
- 🏆 Brands like Maruti, Hyundai, Tata dominate listings  

---

## 📈 Visualizations Used
- Histograms – Used to understand the distribution of numerical features like car prices  
- Bar Plots – Used to compare categorical data such as top car brands and cities  
- Line Plots – Used to analyze trends over time (e.g., price vs year)  
- Pie Charts – Used to show proportion of categories like ownership distribution  

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This analysis shows that price is influenced by ownership, location, brand, and age of the car.  

- Buyers prefer single-owner and newer cars  
- Sellers can price better using market trends  
- Cities significantly impact pricing  
- The market is dominated by mid-range vehicles
